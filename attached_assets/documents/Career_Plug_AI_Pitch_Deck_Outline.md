## Slide 2: The Problem
* **Headline:** The Disconnect in Early Talent
* **Key Points:**
  * **High Unemployment + Skills Gap:** South Africa has massive youth unemployment, yet corporations struggle to find talent in scarce-skill sectors (Tech, Finance, Sciences).
  * **Lack of Guidance:** Overburdened teachers (high student-to-counselor ratios) cannot provide personalized career mapping.
  * **The Digital Divide:** Students lack the data and devices required to research modern careers or build digital portfolios.
  * **Invisible Talent:** Top-performing students in underserved areas remain completely invisible to the market until they reach university.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
* **Headline:** The Career Hub, Reimagined.
* **Visual:** Close-up of the Kiosk interface showing the Career Map.
* **Key Points:**
  * We deploy standalone, interactive Touchscreen Kiosks directly into school foyers and community hubs.
  * An offline-first, data-free career management system.
  * Connects high-potential learners to modern careers without requiring a smartphone, app download, or internet data.

---

## Slide 4: How It Works (The Student Journey)
* **Headline:** A Frictionless, 5-Minute Experience
* **Visual:** A 4-step flowchart or icons.
* **Key Points:**
  1. **Discover:** Gamified strengths assessment on the kiosk touchscreen.
  2. **Match:** Instant AI matching with 79 high-demand, modern career pathways.
  3. **Build:** Students instantly generate a "Mini Portfolio" highlighting their skills.
  4. **Export:** Secure export via WhatsApp QR code straight to a basic smartphone or parent's phone—zero data required.

---

## Slide 5: The Value Proposition (B2B & B2B2C)
* **Headline:** A Win-Win-Win Ecosystem
* **Key Points:**
  * **For Corporates (e.g., Sanlam):** Early talent identification. Identify high-aptitude students in Grade 9-11 for targeted bursaries and build a measurable, data-driven CSI/ESG pipeline.
  * **For Schools:** Zero administrative burden, no Wi-Fi required, and aggregated data on student career interests to tailor curriculums.
  * **For Students:** Clarity, direction, and a professional digital footprint before they even leave high school.

---

## Slide 6: The Market Opportunity
* **Headline:** Tapping into the African Youth Dividend
* **Key Points:**
  * **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** Millions of high school students across South Africa (and eventually Sub-Saharan Africa) entering the workforce.
  * **Corporate Spend:** Millions of Rands spent annually on Corporate Social Investment (CSI) and early talent recruitment with low ROI and poor tracking.
  * We are positioned at the exact intersection of EdTech, HR Tech, and Corporate ESG.

## Slide 7: Business & Revenue Model
* **Headline:** Sustainable & Scalable
* **Key Points:**
  * **Corporate Sponsorships (B2B):** Companies fund kiosk deployments in target districts in exchange for early talent data insights and exclusive branding ("Powered by Sanlam").
  * **Premium Portals:** Paid access for recruiters, universities, and scouts to search aggregated, anonymized talent pools.
  * **School Licensing:** Subsidized models for private or well-resourced public schools wanting the Counselor Dashboard.
