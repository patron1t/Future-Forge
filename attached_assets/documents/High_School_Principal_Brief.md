# Career Plug AI: Kiosk Pilot Program for High Schools

**To:** The Principal and School Governing Body (SGB)
**Objective:** Introducing the Career Plug AI Touchscreen Kiosk to empower your learners with modern, personalized career guidance—at zero cost to the school or your staff.

## 1. The Vision: Empowering Every Learner
Every learner in your school has unique strengths, but it is impossible for educators to provide personalized career mapping for hundreds of students simultaneously. Furthermore, many learners are unaware of the modern career paths available to them, or they lack the data/devices to research them at home.

**Career Plug AI** solves this by bringing the career center directly to your students.

## 2. What is the Career Plug AI Kiosk?
We are offering to place a sleek, modern **Interactive Touchscreen Kiosk** in your school's foyer, library, or safe common area. It is a standalone "career hub" designed specifically for South African learners.

**The Student Experience (5-10 Minutes):**
1. **Discover:** Learners walk up to the screen and take a quick, gamified strengths assessment.
2. **Match:** The AI instantly matches them with top career paths (from Engineering and Medicine to Software Development and Trades).
3. **Build:** They create a "Mini Portfolio" highlighting their skills and dream careers.
4. **Take Home:** They scan a QR code to securely send their career action plan to their (or their parents') WhatsApp—requiring zero data or airtime on their end.

## 3. Why Host a Kiosk at Your School?
We know your staff is incredibly busy. The kiosk is designed to be completely frictionless for the school administration.

* **Zero Burden on Teachers:** The kiosk is entirely self-serve. Students use it during breaks or after school without requiring supervision or passwords.
* **No Wi-Fi Required:** The kiosk operates independently. It does not need to connect to the school's internet or network infrastructure.
* **Data-Driven Insights for the School:** As the Principal, you receive access to a secure "Counselor Dashboard." This provides you with aggregated, anonymized data showing the top career interests of your student body, helping you tailor your subject offerings and extra-curricular programs.
* **Modernizing the Campus:** The vertical, digital signage kiosk instantly modernizes your school's environment, showing parents and visitors that the school is investing in future-focused technology.

## 4. The Pilot Details
We are currently selecting forward-thinking schools for our fully-funded pilot program.

* **Cost:** Zero. The kiosk hardware, software, delivery, and maintenance are fully covered by our corporate sponsors.
* **Space Required:** Just one standard electrical plug point and 1 square meter of floor space in a supervised, indoor area.
* **Duration:** A 3-month initial pilot, with the option to make it permanent based on student engagement.
