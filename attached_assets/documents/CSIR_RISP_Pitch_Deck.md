## 2. Innovation Description & Specification
* **The Core Problem:** South Africa faces high youth unemployment and a corporate skills gap. Yet, millions of high-potential learners in under-resourced schools are invisible to the market because they lack personalized career guidance, data, and digital devices.
* **The Innovation:** Career Plug AI brings the career center directly to the learners. We deploy standalone, interactive Touchscreen Kiosks into school foyers.
* **Specifications & Features:**
  * **Frictionless UX:** Learners take a 5-minute gamified strengths assessment directly on the kiosk.
  * **AI Matching:** Instantly matches learners to 79 modern career pathways (from AI Engineers to Artisans) based on their unique aptitude.
  * **Zero Data Cost:** The system generates a "Mini Portfolio" and securely exports it to the learner's (or parent's) WhatsApp via a dynamic QR code—requiring zero airtime or app installation from the learner.
  * **Self-Contained:** Designed for the South African context, requiring no school Wi-Fi or teacher supervision.

## 3. Market Information

## 4. Technical Information
* **Software Architecture:** Built on a modern web stack (React/Vite) running in a secure, locked-down "Kiosk OS" environment.
* **Offline-First Capability:** The kiosk application caches critical data locally, allowing uninterrupted operation even during connectivity dips.
* **Data Transfer Protocol:** Instead of requiring students to log in with emails they don't check, the system uses dynamic QR code generation (`qrcode.react`) to pre-fill a WhatsApp message. This routes through a central Career Plug AI WhatsApp business number, delivering PDFs and career maps straight to their phones.
* **Hardware Profile:** Commercial-grade, vertical digital signage touchscreens that only require a standard 220V power outlet. Internal cellular routers manage periodic secure data syncing to the cloud.
