# Career Plug AI: Partnership Brief for Sanlam

**Target:** Corporate Social Investment (CSI), Innovation, and Early Talent Acquisition
**Objective:** Pilot fully-funded Career Plug AI Kiosks in under-resourced South African high schools to build a measurable, tech-enabled early talent pipeline.

## 1. The Challenge: The Disconnect in Early Talent
South Africa faces a critical paradox: a high youth unemployment rate alongside corporations struggling to find young, qualified talent in scarce-skill sectors (Actuarial Science, Data Science, Finance, and Tech). 

The root cause starts early. Millions of high-potential learners in under-resourced schools lack access to:
1. **Personalized Career Guidance:** Overburdened teachers cannot provide 1-on-1 career mapping.
2. **Digital Access:** Smartphone penetration is high, but data costs exclude learners from discovering modern career paths.
3. **Visibility to Employers:** Corporations have no visibility into top-performing Grade 9-11 learners until they reach university—which is often too late to guide their subject choices.

## 2. The Solution: The Career Plug AI Kiosk
Career Plug AI is a career management system built specifically for the South African context. To bridge the digital divide, we deploy standalone, interactive **Touchscreen Kiosks** directly into school foyers and community hubs.

**How the Kiosk Works (Zero Data Cost for Learners):**
* **Instant Assessment:** Learners complete a 5-minute interactive strengths assessment on the sleek, vertical touchscreen.
* **AI-Driven Matching:** The system instantly matches their strengths to a database of 79 modern career paths (from Actuaries to AI Engineers).
* **Offline-First Export:** Results and "Mini Portfolios" are securely exported via a scanned WhatsApp QR code or a unique reference PIN—meaning learners don't need airtime or a smartphone to retain their data.

## 3. The Sanlam ROI (Why Partner With Us)
Sponsoring a pilot of Career Plug AI Kiosks is not just a CSI donation; it is a measurable investment in Sanlam's future talent pipeline.

**A. Early Talent Identification (The "Moneyball" Approach)**
By placing kiosks in targeted schools, Sanlam gains aggregated, anonymized data on the career trajectories of thousands of learners. When high-aptitude learners matching Sanlam's scarce-skill needs (e.g., Mathematics, Accounting, Tech) are identified early, Sanlam can nurture them through targeted bursaries *before* they even write Matric.

**B. Unprecedented Brand Visibility**
The physical kiosks (modern, vertical digital signage) act as permanent, interactive billboards within the school. 
* Co-branded "Powered by Sanlam" screens ensure Sanlam is the first corporate brand these learners interact with when thinking about their future.
* WhatsApp exports carry the Sanlam branding directly onto the learner's (or their parents') personal devices.

**C. Measurable, Data-Driven CSI Impact**
Unlike traditional career days, the kiosk provides exact metrics:
* How many learners completed assessments?
* What are the most popular career interests in specific districts?
* How many female learners are opting for STEM career tracks?
* This data provides robust, transparent reporting for Sanlam's ESG and CSI scorecards.
